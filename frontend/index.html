Extended IPA & Creature Bioacoustics Reference

1. Vowels & Resonances

Standard open/close front/back vowels with $F_1-F_5$ formants (e.g., i, y, e, eps [ɛ], ae [æ], a, schwa [ə], u, o, open_o [ɔ], turn_m [ɯ], nasal_a [ã]).

2. Non-Pulmonic Clicks & Ejectives

  • click_dental [ǀ]: Sharp high "tsk-tsk" suction click.
  • click_alveolar [ǃ]: Hollow, popping alveolar click.
  • click_lateral [ǁ]: Side-tongue suction pop.
  • click_bilabial [ʘ]: Soft lip-smack click.
  • ejective_k [kʼ], ejective_t [tʼ], ejective_p [pʼ]: Glottalic egressive pressure bursts.
  • implosive_b [ɓ], implosive_d [ɗ]: Glottalic ingressive pre-voiced stops.

3. Animal & Creature Vocalizations

  • feline_purr: 24.5 Hz rhythmic laryngeal neural twitch gating respiration.
  • feline_growl: Subharmonic ventricular false-cord vibration with deep throat resonance.
  • feline_hiss: High-intensity velic/pharyngeal turbulent jet (3.5–7.5 kHz).
  • canine_snarl: Aggressive lip-curl mucosal flutter (48 Hz) with upper-mid brightness.
  • canine_bark: Chest-impact impulse with rapid pitch decay.
  • canine_whine: High falsetto harmonic series with pitch glides (800–2500 Hz).
  • canine_howl: Sustained resonant fundamental sweep with slow vibrato.
